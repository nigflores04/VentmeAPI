# AI Interior Design App

This project is an AI-powered tool for designing and visualizing interior spaces.  
Users can upload photos of their rooms, describe their vision, and receive realistic AI-generated design variations.

---

## Features & Flow

### 1. Onboarding

- User lands on the app and sees **"Design Your Room / Create Design / Design Space"** CTA.
- Options: **Log in as Guest** or **Sign Up** (optional).
  - Guests get limited design options and results.
- Role selection:
  - **Individual**
  - **Decor enthusiast**
  - **Professional designer**
  - **Design agency**

---

### 2. Start Design

- User uploads:
  - **One photo** of their empty (or current) room
  - **Text prompt** (ideas/instructions)
  - **Room type** (Bedroom, Living room, Office, Studio, etc.)
  - **Style preset** (Minimalist, Cozy, Modern, Scandinavian, Industrial, etc.)
- AI processes the image, prompt, and chosen options.

---

### 3. AI-Generated Designs

- AI generates **3–4 realistic variations** of the room.
- User can:
  - Swipe/scroll to compare results
  - Tap **"Regenerate"** for fine-tuned edits (e.g., _“Make it brighter”_, _“Try wooden floors”_)
  - Select and export favorite design(s)
- All designs, prompts, and assets are **auto-saved** into a project folder.

---

### 4. Basic Product Catalog _(Optional Lite MVP)_

- For each AI-generated variation, the user can tap on furniture (sofa, desk, bed, etc.).
- App suggests **1–2 real product options** (basic catalog integration).

---

### 5. Export & Plan

- Export **high-quality images** for personal use or sharing.
- Export a simple **moodboard** (colors + furniture list, optional).
